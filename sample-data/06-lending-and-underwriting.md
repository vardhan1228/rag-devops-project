# Lending Products and Underwriting Criteria

Rates below are indicative and effective 1 April 2026. Every sanction is subject
to credit assessment; meeting the stated minimums does not create an entitlement
to a loan.

## Reference rate

Retail floating rate loans are linked to the external benchmark, the policy repo
rate, plus a spread. The spread has two parts: a credit risk premium reflecting
the borrower's assessed risk, and a fixed operating spread. The operating spread
cannot be changed during the life of the loan. The credit risk premium may be
revised only on a material deterioration in the borrower's credit assessment, and
not more than once in three years.

Rate resets follow the benchmark on the first day of each quarter.

## Home loan

Amounts from five lakh to five crore, tenures up to thirty years subject to the
borrower's age at maturity not exceeding seventy years.

Loan to value is capped by property value: 90 percent for property up to thirty
lakh, 80 percent from thirty lakh to seventy-five lakh, and 75 percent above
seventy-five lakh. These ceilings exclude stamp duty and registration, which the
borrower funds separately.

Interest from the repo rate plus 2.15 percent for salaried applicants with a
credit score of 775 and above. Processing fee 0.35 percent of the sanctioned
amount, minimum 5,000, maximum 25,000.

No prepayment charge on floating rate loans to individuals, whether prepaid
partially or fully, and whatever the source of funds. Fixed rate loans prepaid
from sources other than the borrower's own income attract 2 percent of the amount
prepaid.

## Loan against property

Up to 60 percent of the market value of a self-occupied residential or commercial
property. Tenure up to fifteen years. Interest from the repo rate plus 3.40
percent. The end use must be declared and cannot be speculative investment in
capital markets.

## Personal loan

Unsecured, fifty thousand to forty lakh, tenures twelve to seventy-two months.
Interest from 10.75 percent to 21.00 percent fixed, set by assessed risk.
Processing fee up to 2 percent of the sanctioned amount.

Minimum net monthly income of 25,000 in metro locations and 18,000 elsewhere.
Minimum credit score of 720. Applicants with an enquiry count above six in the
preceding three months are declined irrespective of score, since dense enquiry
activity signals distress borrowing.

Foreclosure permitted after twelve instalments, charged at 4 percent of the
outstanding principal.

## Vehicle loan

New cars up to 90 percent of the on-road price, tenures up to seven years,
interest from 9.10 percent. Used cars up to 75 percent of the valuation, vehicle
age plus tenure not exceeding ten years, interest from 12.50 percent.

The vehicle is hypothecated to the bank and the hypothecation is recorded with the
transport authority. Release of the charge is issued within fifteen days of
closure.

## Gold loan

Up to 75 percent of the value of the pledged ornaments, assessed on the prevailing
rate for twenty-two carat gold, net of stone and making weight. Tenure up to
twenty-four months. Interest from 9.25 percent.

Where the loan to value breaches 78 percent because of a fall in gold prices, the
borrower is given thirty days to part-prepay or pledge additional security before
auction proceedings begin. Auction requires two weeks' notice and is conducted
publicly.

## Assessment

**Fixed obligation to income ratio.** Total monthly obligations including the
proposed instalment divided by net monthly income. The ceiling is 50 percent for
income up to one lakh per month, 60 percent above that. Existing credit card dues
are counted at 5 percent of the outstanding balance.

**Credit score.** Sourced from the bureaux at application. Below 650 the
application is declined for unsecured products. A borrower with no credit history
is assessed on banking conduct and income stability instead of being declined
outright.

**Income verification.** Salaried applicants provide three months of salary slips
and six months of bank statements. Self-employed applicants provide two years of
tax returns with computation, and audited financial statements where turnover
exceeds the audit threshold.

**Property valuation.** Two independent valuations for property above one crore,
with the lower of the two adopted. The legal opinion on title must be clear;
conditional opinions require the conditions to be satisfied before disbursement.

## Default and recovery

An account is classified as a special mention account at thirty-one days overdue,
and as non-performing at ninety-one days overdue. Classification is at the
borrower level, so one defaulting facility affects all facilities of that
borrower.

Recovery follows a graduated path: reminders, a notice of demand, and only then
enforcement. Recovery agents may contact borrowers between eight in the morning
and seven in the evening, must identify themselves and their agency, and may not
contact family members other than a co-borrower or guarantor. Any use of
intimidation is grounds for terminating the agency.

Enforcement under the securitisation act requires a sixty day notice specifying
the amount and the intended action, and the borrower may make representations
which must be answered with reasons within fifteen days.
