# Schedule of Fees and Charges

Effective 1 April 2026. All amounts in rupees and exclusive of Goods and
Services Tax, which is levied at 18 percent on the charge amount.

## Balance shortfall

Charged monthly when the monthly average balance falls below the product
requirement. The charge is graduated by the size of the shortfall.

| Shortfall as share of requirement | Charge per month |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 50 percent | 150 |
| Above 50 percent | 400 |

No shortfall charge applies to Basic Savings Bank Deposit Accounts, Zero Balance
Salary Accounts while active, or accounts of customers aged seventy-five and
above.

## Cash handling at branches

Five free cash transactions per month, then 150 per transaction. Cash deposits
above two lakh per day at a non-home branch attract 0.50 per 1,000 subject to a
minimum of 150.

## ATM usage

Five free transactions per month at Meridian ATMs, including balance enquiries.
Three free transactions per month at other banks' ATMs in metro locations and
five in non-metro locations.

Beyond the free limit, 23 per financial transaction and 10 per non-financial
transaction. A declined transaction due to insufficient funds is charged 25 and
is not counted against the free limit.

## Debit cards

| Item | Charge |
| --- | --- |
| Annual fee, RuPay platinum | 250 |
| Annual fee, Visa signature | 750 |
| Replacement, damaged or lost | 300 |
| PIN regeneration at branch | 50 |
| International transaction markup | 3.50 percent of amount |

## Cheques

Fifty free leaves per financial year on Regular Savings Accounts, then 4 per
leaf. Stop payment instruction is 100 per instrument or 200 for a range.

An inward cheque returned for insufficient funds is charged 350 for the first
return in a quarter and 750 for each subsequent return. An outward cheque
returned by the drawee bank is charged 150.

## Electronic payments

Unified Payments Interface transactions are free for customers. National
Electronic Funds Transfer and Immediate Payment Service transactions initiated
through internet or mobile banking are free.

Real Time Gross Settlement initiated at a branch is charged 25 for amounts from
two lakh to five lakh and 50 above five lakh. Transactions initiated through
digital channels are free.

## Statements and certificates

Monthly electronic statement is free. A physical statement posted to the
registered address is 100 per request. A duplicate statement older than twelve
months is 200. Interest certificates and balance confirmation letters are 150
each.

## Standing instructions and mandates

Registration is free. A failed standing instruction due to insufficient funds is
charged 200. A failed electronic mandate for a loan instalment is charged 500,
which is the regulatory maximum for such returns.

## Locker rent

Annual rent by locker size, payable in advance: small 1,500, medium 3,000, large
6,000, extra large 10,000. A fixed deposit equal to three years of rent plus
break-open charges is required as security. Rent is not refundable if the locker
is surrendered mid-year.

## Account closure

Free if closed within fourteen days of opening or after twelve months. Closure
between fifteen days and twelve months attracts 500.

## Charges the bank does not levy

No charge is made for crediting interest, for inward remittances, for updating
Know Your Customer records, for registering a nominee, or for closing an account
on the death of the account holder.
