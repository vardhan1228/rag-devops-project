## Complaint escalation

Complaints move through three internal levels before any external forum.

**Level one, branch or contact centre.** Acknowledged immediately with a unique
complaint reference number. Resolution target ten working days.

**Level two, nodal officer.** Available if level one has not resolved the matter
within ten working days, or if the response is unsatisfactory. Resolution target a
further ten working days.

**Level three, principal nodal officer.** The bank's final internal authority.
Resolution target thirty days from the original complaint date.

Every response at every level states the outcome, the reasoning, and the next
escalation available. A complaint is never closed as resolved without the customer
being told what the resolution is.

## Reserve Bank Integrated Ombudsman

A customer may approach the Ombudsman if the bank rejects the complaint, if the
reply is unsatisfactory, or if no reply is received within thirty days of the
complaint. The scheme covers deficiency in service generally rather than a fixed
list of grounds.

Filing is free. There is no fee at any stage and no lawyer is required.

The Ombudsman may award compensation for loss suffered up to twenty lakh, and
separately up to one lakh for mental agony and harassment including the time and
expense the complainant incurred.

A complaint is not entertained where the matter is already before a court or
another forum, where it is time barred beyond one year from the bank's reply, or
where it concerns a commercial judgement the bank is entitled to make, such as
declining a loan on credit grounds.

## Deceased account claims

Where a valid nomination exists, the balance is paid to the nominee on
production of proof of death and the nominee's identification. A succession
certificate is not required and must not be demanded.

Where no nomination exists and the balance does not exceed five lakh, payment may
be made against an indemnity with two sureties, at the discretion of the sanctioning
authority.

Settlement is completed within fifteen working days of receiving complete
documents. Interest at the savings rate continues to accrue until the date of
settlement.

## Dormant and inactive accounts

An account with no customer induced transaction for twelve months is classified
inactive, and for twenty-four months dormant. Interest credits do not count as
customer induced.

Reactivation requires a request and current identification, is completed within
three working days, and carries no charge. Balances in accounts dormant for ten
years are transferred to the depositor education and awareness fund, and remain
claimable by the customer or their legal heirs indefinitely thereafter.

## Right to be informed

Any change in fees, charges or terms is notified at least thirty days before it
takes effect. Where a change is adverse and the customer does not accept it, the
account may be closed within sixty days of the notice without any closure charge.

Interest rate changes on savings accounts take effect prospectively only.

## Accessibility

Branches maintain ramp access, and customers with disabilities are attended
without queueing. Doorstep banking for cash pickup, cheque collection and
document delivery is provided at no charge to customers above seventy years and to
customers with a disability certificate.
