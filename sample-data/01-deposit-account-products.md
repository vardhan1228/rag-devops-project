# Deposit Account Products

Meridian Retail Bank offers five deposit products to individual customers. All
figures are in Indian rupees. Interest rates are reviewed quarterly and the
rates below are effective from 1 April 2026.

## Regular Savings Account

Minimum monthly average balance of 10,000 in metro and urban branches, 5,000 in
semi-urban branches, and 2,500 in rural branches. Falling below the requirement
attracts a shortfall charge described in the fees schedule.

Interest is 2.70 percent per annum on balances up to 50 lakh and 3.00 percent
above that. Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited
quarterly, on the last working day of June, September, December and March.

Includes a RuPay platinum debit card, unlimited UPI transactions, and fifty free
cheque leaves per financial year.

## Zero Balance Salary Account

Available to employees of companies with an active salary arrangement. No
minimum balance requirement while salary credits continue. If no salary is
credited for three consecutive months the account converts to a Regular Savings
Account and the balance requirement begins to apply from the following month.

Interest matches the Regular Savings Account. Debit card annual fee is waived
for the life of the account.

## Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account

A financial inclusion product with no minimum balance and no charge for
non-maintenance. Limited to four cash withdrawals per month including ATM
withdrawals. Deposits are unrestricted.

A customer may hold only one Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account across the bank.
Holding this product bars the customer from holding any other savings account
at Meridian.

## Fixed Deposit

Tenures from seven days to ten years. Minimum deposit 5,000, in multiples of
1,000 thereafter.

Rates for deposits below two crore, general public:

| Tenure | Rate per annum |
| --- | --- |
| 7 to 45 days | 3.50 percent |
| 46 to 179 days | 5.25 percent |
| 180 to 364 days | 6.40 percent |
| 1 year to 2 years | 7.10 percent |
| 2 years 1 day to 3 years | 7.00 percent |
| 3 years 1 day to 5 years | 6.75 percent |
| Above 5 years to 10 years | 6.50 percent |

Senior citizens aged sixty and above receive an additional 0.50 percent on all
tenures of 180 days and above.

Premature closure is permitted with a penalty of 1.00 percent on the applicable
rate for the period the deposit was actually held. Deposits booked under the Tax
Saver scheme carry a mandatory five year lock-in and cannot be closed early
except on the death of the depositor.

## Recurring Deposit

Monthly instalments from 500 upward, tenures of twelve to one hundred and twenty
months. The rate applicable to the matching Fixed Deposit tenure applies and is
locked for the full term at the time of opening.

A missed instalment attracts a penalty of 1.50 per 100 per month for the period
of delay. Six consecutive missed instalments cause the account to be closed and
the balance paid out at the savings rate.

## Deposit insurance

Deposits are insured by the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation
up to five lakh per depositor per bank, covering principal and interest together.
The cover applies to the aggregate across all branches, not per account.
