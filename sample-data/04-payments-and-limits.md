# Payments, Transfer Limits and Settlement Timings

Four rails are available for moving money between accounts. Choosing the right
one depends on amount, urgency and whether the beneficiary bank is open.

## Unified Payments Interface

Immediate, available every day of the year including bank holidays. Funds move
within seconds and the payer receives confirmation in the application.

Per transaction limit is one lakh for most categories. The limit rises to two
lakh for capital markets, insurance premium and travel bookings, and to five lakh
for tax payments and for payments to hospitals and educational institutions.

A maximum of twenty transactions per account per day applies, and a cumulative
limit of one lakh per day where no higher category limit applies. A newly linked
account is restricted to 5,000 for the first twenty-four hours.

Reversal of a successful payment is not possible. A payment credited to the wrong
beneficiary must be pursued as a dispute against that beneficiary.

## Immediate Payment Service

Immediate, available every day including holidays. Per transaction limit is five
lakh through internet and mobile banking, and 25,000 at a branch counter.

Requires the beneficiary account number and the eleven character Indian
Financial System Code identifying the branch. Unlike the Unified Payments
Interface there is no per-day transaction count restriction.

## National Electronic Funds Transfer

Available around the clock every day, settled in half-hourly batches. There is no
minimum or maximum limit imposed by the system; Meridian applies a per
transaction cap of ten lakh through digital channels.

Credit to the beneficiary is expected within thirty minutes of the batch in which
the transaction settles. A transaction that cannot be credited must be returned
by the beneficiary bank within two hours of the batch completing.

## Real Time Gross Settlement

For high value transfers, minimum two lakh, no upper limit. Settled individually
and immediately rather than in batches. Available around the clock every day.

Preferred for property transactions and other settlements where proof of
immediate final settlement is required.

## Cooling period for new beneficiaries

A newly added beneficiary may receive up to 50,000 in the first twenty-four hours
after registration. The full limit applies thereafter. The cooling period cannot
be waived by the branch or the contact centre; it is a fraud control.

## Cheque clearing

Cheques are cleared through image based truncation. A cheque deposited before the
branch cut-off is presented the same working day and funds are available on the
next working day. Deposits after cut-off are presented the following working day.

Cheques for one lakh and above are subject to positive pay: the issuer must
register the date, beneficiary name and amount through internet banking, mobile
banking or the contact centre before presentation. An unregistered high value
cheque may be returned.

## Failed transactions and automatic compensation

Where a transaction fails after the account has been debited, the reversal
timelines below apply. Compensation accrues automatically without the customer
having to ask.

| Scenario | Reversal deadline | Compensation if missed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unified Payments Interface, debit without credit | T plus 1 day | 100 per day |
| Card transaction, debit without success | T plus 5 days | 100 per day |
| Automated teller machine, cash not dispensed | T plus 5 days | 100 per day |
| National Electronic Funds Transfer, credit not applied | 2 hours from batch | 100 per day |

T is the date of the transaction. Compensation is credited to the same account
and is visible in the statement as a distinct narration.

## Standing instructions on cards

Recurring card mandates above 15,000 require additional authentication for every
debit. Below that threshold, the first debit requires authentication and
subsequent debits proceed on the registered mandate, with a pre-debit
notification sent at least twenty-four hours in advance.
